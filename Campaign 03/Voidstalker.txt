The creature is a bipedal, predatory horror standing approximately 2.8–3.1 meters tall at the shoulder when fully upright, though it habitually crouches low in a digitigrade stance that keeps its center of mass forward and ready to spring. Its body is powerfully muscled yet sleek, built for explosive speed and lethal agility rather than raw bulk.
Head & Skull:
The head is elongated and sharply angular, dominated by a smooth, metallic-silver carapace that forms a sweeping, blade-like crest sweeping back from the brow. A single vivid orange-red triangular accent marks the forward edge of this crest. The lower face is a vicious maw filled with rows of needle-sharp, serrated white teeth; the jaw can hinge open disturbingly wide, exposing a deep red interior and a glistening tongue. Small, predatory red eyes sit deep in protective sockets, giving it a cold, calculating stare. The overall head shape strongly evokes a xenomorph-style biomechanical predator fused with reptilian ferocity.
Torso & Limbs:
The torso is lean and athletic, covered in glossy black-to-deep-purple skin that transitions into segmented, bone-white exoskeletal plating along the spine and shoulders. These plates are smooth, slightly reflective, and segmented like armored vertebrae. The arms are long and powerful, ending in large, clawed hands with four fingers (three forward, one opposable thumb) tipped in long, curved silver claws. The legs are heavily muscled, digitigrade, with reverse-jointed knees and feet that feature three thick, clawed toes plus a smaller rear dewclaw. Silver-white armored guards wrap the shins and forearms, adding to the biomechanical aesthetic.
Tail:
A long, thick, whip-like tail extends from the base of the spine, segmented with the same white bony plating along its upper length. In the first image it curls dramatically; in the second it stretches out straight for balance during a low sprint. The tail ends in a vicious, sickle-shaped blade or hook.
Additional Features (most visible in the concept sheet):

The back can unfurl or split into four to six long, prehensile tentacles (the “back made up of 4 grooves that the tentacles beat in”). These tentacles are dark, sinuous, and end in sharp hooks or grasping tips. They serve as extra limbs for locomotion, climbing, striking, or pinning prey.
The skin has a subtle iridescent sheen under certain lighting, shifting between deep violet, gunmetal black, and blood-red highlights.
Small white sensor-like nodules or ridges run along the spine and joints.
The overall color palette is dominated by glossy black/purple flesh, stark white/silver exoskeletal armor, and that single striking orange-red crest accent.

Movement & Posture:
It moves with terrifying fluidity — low to the ground in a prowling crouch, then exploding into bursts of speed on all fours or bipedal lunges. The tentacles can lash out independently, wrap around obstacles, or help stabilize the body during sharp turns. When agitated or attacking, the crest glows faintly, the maw opens impossibly wide, and the tail whips like a living weapon.
