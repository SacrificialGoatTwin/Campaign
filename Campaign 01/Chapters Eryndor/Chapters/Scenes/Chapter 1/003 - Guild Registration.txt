Scene Title: MAKING YOURSELF LEGAL

The hall exhales around you—boots scuff, tankards clink, a laugh dies halfway when it shouldn’t. Ink waits.

👤 JAYSON | deliberate calm [🦊🧥]

💬 “I have come to this land from across the water.”
💬 “I have no papers or identification, and I would like to correct that.”
💬 “I was told the Adventurers’ Guild is where one starts to make oneself… legal.”

📝 You nod once and draw your hood back just enough—human face only. Nothing extra. Nothing denied.

🧑‍💼 GUILD CLERK | weighing [📜👁️]

📝 His gaze sharpens—not surprised, not impressed. He reaches for a different ledger, thicker, edges darkened by years of first chances and bad ideas.

💬 “You were told correctly.”

📝 He wets the quill, taps twice—ritual, not habit.

💬 “No papers means Provisional Entry.”
💬 “Name you give becomes the name we keep.”
💬 “Lie now and it follows you.”

📝 A glance at your face again, measuring how still you keep it.

💬 “Entry fee is coin or service.”
💬 “Service means your first contract is assigned.”
💬 “Refusal voids the attempt.”

🗂️ THE LEDGER | patient [📘⚖️]

📝 The book opens to a blank page that has swallowed many kinds of people.

💬 “You’ll start unranked.”
💬 “Badge limits where you go and what you’re paid.”
💬 “You earn your way out of it.”

📝 He slides the ledger closer, quill poised.

💬 “State your name.”
💬 “Choose coin… or service.”

The Spark stays quiet—no heat, no flare—only that steady, counting attention.

This is a gate too.
Just built of ink.

Scene Title: INK AND IRONY

The quill stills. Somewhere behind you, a chair scrapes. Someone snorts into a cup and pretends it wasn’t them.

👤 JAYSON | dry humor [🦊🧥]

💬 “My name is Jayson.”
💬 “I’ll take service for my first task.”
💬 “Unfortunately, Harrowfen wasn’t my first stop.”
💬 “A group of bandits was.”
💬 “My coin has now…”
💬 “…switched owners.”

📝 You pause just long enough to let the implication land, then chuckle softly—no bravado, just fact.

🧑‍💼 GUILD CLERK | unimpressed [📜😐]

📝 The quill resumes. Scratch. Scratch. He does not look up.

💬 “Bandits usually say the same thing.”
💬 “Difference is they ask for forgiveness.”

📝 He dots the name with a firm tap.

💬 “Jayson. Provisional.”

📝 A hand reaches under the desk. Wood slides. A small iron badge thumps onto the counter—unranked, plain, honest.

💬 “Service it is.”

🧾 GUILD BOARD | deciding [📌⚖️]

📝 He turns the ledger, exposing a narrow strip of postings marked with a black thread—work no one fights over.

💬 “Road-adjacent task.”
💬 “Low pay. High nuisance.”

📝 He tears a slip cleanly and pushes it across.

💬 “Caravan out of Harrowfen lost two guards yesterday.”
💬 “Didn’t return from the east spur—same woods you came through.”

📝 His eyes lift now. They measure, then narrow.

💬 “Witnesses report ‘clicking’ in the mist.”
💬 “Merchants want confirmation. Families want bodies.”

📝 A beat.

💬 “You bring back proof of cause.”
💬 “If any guards live, you escort them home.”
💬 “If not—tokens.”

🗡️ THE HALL | murmuring [👂🗺️]

📝 A few heads turn. Someone mutters Listeners. Someone else shakes theirs like that’s bad luck.

📝 The Spark tightens—no flare—just recognition. Risk. Intent. Outcome. The shape of the rules you were warned about.

💬 “Badge stays visible,” the clerk adds.
💬 “You don’t freelance justice.”
💬 “You report.”

📝 He pushes a charcoal stub toward you.

💬 “Mark acceptance.”

Ink waits.
So does the road—again.
