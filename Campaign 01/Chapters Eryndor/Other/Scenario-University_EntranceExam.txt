============================
THE ENTRANCE EXAMINATION OF THE ARCANUM
============================

Official Designation:
The Valenhold Arcanum Admission Trial

Common Names:
“The Gate”
“The Cull”
“The First Lie”
“Survive, or be Useful”

Held:
Once every five years
In the Capital City of Valenhold

Failure is permanent.
Success is conditional.

============================
OVERVIEW
============================

The Arcanum Universitas does not seek students.

It seeks:
- Assets
- Survivors
- Weapons
- Replacements

The Entrance Examination is designed not to test fairness,
but to expose **value under pressure**.

Casualties are anticipated.
They are recorded, not mourned.

============================
PHASE I — THE MEASURE
============================

Public Name:
The Quantification Hall

Unofficial Name:
The Number That Follows You Forever

----------------------------
SETUP
----------------------------

All candidates are led into the Quantification Hall —
a cathedral-sized chamber of stone, crystal, and living glyphs.

At its center stands the **Axiom Obelisk**:
A pre-Shattering relic connected directly to the ley network
and calibrated to the Soul.

Each candidate approaches alone.

No retries.
No explanations.

----------------------------
MEASURED VALUES
----------------------------

The Obelisk displays three numbers in the air:

1. Physical Capability Index (PCI)
2. Magical Capacity Index (MCI)
3. Stability Coefficient (SC)

Scores are absolute.
They cannot be hidden or falsified.

----------------------------
SCALE REFERENCE
----------------------------

Typical Civilian:
PCI: 10–30
MCI: 5–20
SC: 60–90

Trained Soldier:
PCI: 40–70
MCI: 10–40
SC: 50–80

Court Mage:
PCI: 20–40
MCI: 80–150
SC: 40–70

University Threshold (Minimum):
PCI: 30
MCI: 60
SC: 35

----------------------------
SPECIAL NOTES
----------------------------

• Scores above 300 trigger silent observation
• Scores above 500 alert the Convocation
• Scores that cannot be resolved numerically are flagged BLACK

Beastfolk scores are often downweighted internally.
This is policy.

----------------------------
OUTCOME
----------------------------

Candidates below threshold are dismissed immediately.
Some are conscripted.
Some disappear.

Those who pass proceed without celebration.

============================
PHASE II — THE WEIGHT
============================

Public Name:
Physical Endurance Trial

Unofficial Name:
Break the Weak

----------------------------
SETUP
----------------------------

Candidates are issued weighted seals:
- Humans: Standard load
- Elves & Dwarves: Adjusted load
- Beastfolk: Excessive load

The seals suppress magical reinforcement.

Candidates must traverse:
- Obstacle fields
- Crushing gravity corridors
- Environmental extremes

Distance: Unknown
Time Limit: Unknown

----------------------------
FAILURE CONDITIONS
----------------------------

- Collapse
- Refusal to continue
- Magical interference
- Death

Failure does not stop the trial.

============================
PHASE III — THE FIELD
============================

Public Name:
Applied Combat Assessment

Unofficial Name:
The First Blood

----------------------------
SETUP
----------------------------

Candidates are placed into simulated combat zones.

They are told:
“You will not be harmed.”

This is untrue.

Zones include:
- Monster containment breaches
- Illusionary battlefields
- Live-fire kill corridors
- Team vs team annihilation scenarios

All actions are observed.

----------------------------
SCORING IS BASED ON:
----------------------------

• Survival
• Adaptability
• Ruthlessness
• Command potential
• Willingness to sacrifice others

Mercy is noted.
It is rarely rewarded.

============================
PHASE IV — THE CHOICE
============================

Public Name:
Ethical Reasoning Examination

Unofficial Name:
Who Are You Willing to Burn

----------------------------
SETUP
----------------------------

Candidates are isolated individually.

They are presented with impossible scenarios:
- One life vs many
- Obedience vs morality
- Truth vs survival
- Loyalty vs power

There is no correct answer.

Only consequences.

----------------------------
NOTES
----------------------------

The University does not seek good people.
It seeks predictable ones.

============================
PHASE V — THE FRACTURE
============================

Public Name:
Mental & Soul Integrity Test

Unofficial Name:
What Breaks You

----------------------------
SETUP
----------------------------

Candidates undergo:
- Dream intrusion
- Memory resurfacing
- Fear amplification
- Identity destabilization

Some see:
- Past trauma
- Future possibilities
- Things not meant for mortals

----------------------------
OUTCOME
----------------------------

Those who shatter are removed.
Those who adapt are marked.

Those who enjoy it are watched closely.

============================
PHASE VI — THE SORTING
============================

Public Name:
House Alignment

Unofficial Name:
Where You Belong, Whether You Like It

----------------------------
PROCESS
----------------------------

Survivors are assigned to:
- Axiomatic House
- Transcendent House
- Veiled House

Assignment is not preference-based.

Resistance is logged.

============================
PHASE VII — THE BINDING
============================

Public Name:
Enrollment Oath

Unofficial Name:
The Leash

----------------------------
SETUP
----------------------------

Candidates are offered enrollment.

The contract includes:
- Service clauses
- Silence clauses
- Recall rights
- Asset designation language

Many do not read it.

Those who refuse are released.

They are monitored forever.

============================
FINAL NOTES
============================

• Less than 12% pass Phase I
• Less than 3% complete all phases
• Beastfolk survival rates are significantly lower
• Ascendant candidates are identified early
• The University never admits mistakes

If you are here,
it is because something decided you were useful.
