CAMPAIGN SUMMARY
CHAPTER 6 — ERYNDOR
TAGS: [000001] → [000161]
==========================

SETTING & START STATE
---------------------
• Date: 25/04/1427 (Eryndor calendar)
• Time: Morning → Late Night
• Location start: Outside Harrowfen Gate
• Party: Jayson (PC), Kestrel (C-rank), Irix (unranked anomaly)
• Objective: Travel to Grey-moss Depths (fresh C-rank dungeon) to complete Jayon's advancement requirements

Jayson is emotionally stabilized after confirmation that his four wives (Ayaka, Shion, Seika, Lysera) are alive. Irix’s true nature is known only to Jayson at the start of the chapter. Kestrel is unaware initially.

---------------------------------
ROAD TO GREY-MOSS DEPTHS
---------------------------------
• The party leaves Harrowfen calmly.
• They encounter farmers, peddlers, and signs of another adventuring group (B-rank party previously humiliated at the Guild).
• Jayson identifies tracks and scents indicating the B-rank group is nearby.
• A confrontation occurs:
  - The B-rank party initiates aggression.
  - Jayson deliberately provokes them to disrupt coordination.
  - Magic is exchanged (fire, spatial effects); Jayson is visibly excited by spellcasting.
  - Jayson disarms and humiliates the warrior without lethal force.
  - Emotional confrontation reveals the B-rank warrior’s resentment and inferiority complex.
  - Jayson de-escalates philosophically, reframing skill, achievement, and rage.
• A rogue from the B-rank party attempts to take Irix hostage.
• Irix’s hood falls, revealing her non-human nature.
• Jayson intervenes at lethal speed, forcing the rogue to choose between release and death.
• The rogue releases her.
• Jayson publicly rejects racial judgment and protects Irix.
• The B-rank party withdraws shaken; the rogue later deserts them.
• Trust between Kestrel and Irix deepens.

---------------------------------
SLAVER CARAVAN INCIDENT
---------------------------------
• The party encounters a large slaver caravan (≈25 infantry + leadership).
• Slavers show interest in Irix.
• Jayson attempts verbal deterrence.
• Slavers escalate to violence.
• Jayson engages fully:
  - Displays nine tails.
  - Executes ~12 slavers rapidly and precisely.
  - Uses Kestrel’s sword and his katana simultaneously.
• Jayson gives Irix agency:
  - Asks if she wants the rest dead.
  - She chooses mercy.
• Remaining slavers are forced to retreat.
• Slaves are freed, given supplies, instructed to report to the Guild.
• Evidence is destroyed.
• Jayson sustains a bolt wound; healed later by a healer from the B-rank party.
• Guild patrol is notified and informed.
• Reputation impact: Jayson is now a known extreme-force actor.

---------------------------------
GREY-MOSS DEPTHS DUNGEON
---------------------------------
• Party enters dungeon.
• Environmental hazards: slick stone, deep water.
• Jayson carries Irix and Kestrel through water safely.
• Subtle unseen/ethereal threats detected but not engaged.
• Dungeon reacts to **intent** rather than violence.
• Final chamber separates the party:
  - Jayson is isolated with the dungeon “boss” / intelligence.
  - The entity tempts conquest and power.
• Jayson rejects domination.
• He chooses the safety of Kestrel and Irix over dungeon rewards.
• Dungeon releases them unharmed.
• Dungeon considered completed.
• Party exits safely.

---------------------------------
RETURN TO HARROWFEN & GUILD
---------------------------------
• Party reports dungeon completion.
• Jayson receives updated plaque (F-rank progressing).
• Kestrel confirmed C-rank.
• Irix registers with the Guild:
  - Rejects the term “Demon”.
  - Declares a rare, noble lineage (recognized by clerk).
• Unsavory comments are made by bystanders.
• Jayson dismisses them theatrically, reinforcing pride and belonging.
• Irix gains Guild protection status.
• Party shares food and reviews Jayon's menu/“Spark” growth.

---------------------------------
NIGHT INCIDENT — ASSASSINATION ATTEMPT
---------------------------------
• On the way to an inn, unknown agents attempt “verification” of Irix.
• Jayson senses danger and secures four rooms under false allocation.
• Party barricades in a private room.
• Professional assassins infiltrate:
  - At least two high-tier assassins.
  - Several operatives.
  - One leader (“Observer”).
• It is revealed:
  - Irix is a **continuity anchor** — a living reference point stabilizing reality across resets.
  - She is sought by collectors, capitals, and higher systems.
• Jayson blindfolds himself to reduce variables.
• Combat erupts:
  - Extreme close-quarters fighting.
  - Jayson throws one assassin through a window.
  - Kestrel holds the hallway against another.
  - Irix is briefly captured and dragged by her horns.
  - Jayson retrieves his katana intentionally and cuts her free.
• Jayson positions himself as a literal gate between Irix and attackers.
• The Observer attempts withdrawal.
• Jayson throws his katana down the hall:
  - The Observer escapes but loses an ear.
  - Message delivered: “I am coming.”

---------------------------------
AFTERMATH & EMOTIONAL RESOLUTION
---------------------------------
• Guild arrives; scene secured.
• Jayson gives a full, honest report.
• Incident sealed officially.
• Party relocates to a new room.
• After adrenaline fades, Irix breaks down emotionally:
  - Years of running.
  - Fear, exhaustion, resentment of being “important”.
• Jayson physically comforts her:
  - Lifts her into his lap with tails.
  - Includes Kestrel physically and emotionally.
  - Reframes fear as survival, not weakness.
• Jayson acknowledges partial responsibility for exposure.
• Reframes openness as power: enemies are now visible.
• Mood lightens with humor:
  - Teasing about wings.
  - Gentle jokes.
  - Future plans (clothes tailored for Irix, capital travel).
• Trust solidifies:
  - Irix accepts she is no longer alone.
  - Kestrel accepts Irix fully.
• The three fall asleep together safely.
• No further incidents overnight.

---------------------------------
END STATE (FOR NEXT CHAPTER)
---------------------------------
• Party cohesion: STRONG
• Irix status:
  - Guild-registered under noble lineage.
  - Known continuity anchor.
  - Actively targeted by high-level actors.
• Jayson status:
  - Reputation escalated significantly.
  - Known to assassins and higher systems.
  - Spark evolving; power returning in nontraditional ways.
• Kestrel status:
  - Fully committed to the group.
  - Witnessed Jayon's true lethality.
• Narrative threads active:
  - Continuity systems aware of Jayson.
  - Collectors and capitals will respond.
  - “I am coming” message delivered.
  - Capital / University of Magic planned.
  - Irix no longer hiding — protected openly.
