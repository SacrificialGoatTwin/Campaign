CAMPAIGN SUMMARY
CHAPTER 8 — ERYNDOR
“FIRST BELL KNOCK”
TAGS: FUCKED UP BY YOU
==========================

PARTY
-----
• Jayson (PC)
• Kestrel (ally; elf-like ears with limited movement)
• Irix (Serathi; batlike wings, always hidden by default)

==========================
COPPER FINCH INN — FIRST BELL PANIC
==========================
• Morning, raining softly. Jayson is asleep in an impossible upside-down chair knot, tails everywhere, tongue out.
• Guild runner presence/pressure outside (first bell isn’t flexible).
• Jayson slides off the chair with a thud, jolts awake delirious (dumplings / “pond council” nonsense), dressed only in underwear.
• Realization hits: “oh shit, first bell!” Jayson throws on clothes, frantic teeth-brush, ready within minutes.
• Party moves out fast, following the scout/runner toward obligations.

==========================
HARROWFEN — GUILD HALL STATEMENTS → EAST ANNEX MOVE
==========================
• On the walk: Jayson jokes about being carried by Irix’s wings (immediately clarifies he’s kidding).
• Jayson teases Kestrel about calling him “heavy,” does exaggerated “leaf on the wind / thudding cow” antics.
• Arrival at Guild: Jayson does the “tail-spider” gag again (hands Irix a tail leash, spreads the rest like a massive tail-spider, screeches theatrically entering).
• Inside: Jayson greets the Marshal, gives a softened/cleaned version of last night:
  - Late night at the big inn near the central courtyard; insults occurred; it ended in an apology.
  - Jayson admits he baited them and “may have threatened to bite,” but frames it as ending well.
• Jayson pushes for moving straight to East Annex assessments after statements.
• Kestrel discomfort from earlier ear-biting joke is addressed later—Jayson tries to repair the awkwardness and re-open playful banter.

==========================
EAST ANNEX — WAITING CHAMBER TWO (SIGILS + “SPIDER MODE”)
==========================
• Chamber Two is heavily layered with security sigils (detection/damping/containment/witness).
• Jayson hits a sigil layer that drains mood/joy (“suck joy”), complains about how overbuilt the security feels.
• Irix and Kestrel explain the layering logic: overlap reduces false positives and catches subtle threats.
• Jayson continues comedic “spider” performances:
  - Floofy “hug me, I’m a fluffy spider” version.
  - Horror version: using a tail like a spider “butt-grab” dragging him, pretending he’s captured prey.
• A calibration chime occurs in the hall (random turn / system settling), sigils dim back toward baseline.
• An attendant eventually calls Jayson in for assessment.

==========================
EAST ANNEX — JAYSON’S ASSESSMENT (OBSERVATION / CHOICE TEST)
==========================
• Jayson enters an assessment room with high-sigil pressure and a suspicious “stone wall” that feels wrong.
• Jayson demonstrates discipline: doesn’t probe; instead reads airflow/pressure and recognizes the wall is an illusion (air moves through it).
• The assessor frames the next portion as “choices,” and Jayson stands and clears space (expects movement will matter).
• Heat/vibration is detected behind the false wall; Jayson repositions to opposite side, watches both wall and door.
• Ending beat: the false wall “becomes real” (or resolves), impressing Jayson—he stops himself from demanding how, calls it cool, then exits.
• Jayson waits in the lobby while Kestrel and Irix take their assessments.

==========================
ANNEX LOBBY → INN BREAKFAST (RESULTS + VIBE SHIFT)
==========================
• After a wait (~40 minutes), the party reunites; Jayson asks if they passed, gets excited, nearly hugs/tackles but stops himself (cultural adjustment).
• Clerk warns them about “something” (implied looming trouble / capital / politics), but they proceed to the inn opposite the Annex for breakfast.
• More adventurer groups filter in—many racist/snooty human groups, celebration tables, failed applicants, post-exam tension.
• The previous hostile A-rank adventurer group from Harrowfen appears—still intact, apparently also passed / moving on to university.

==========================
APOLOGY PLAY + BAR ESCALATION (SOCIAL CONTROL THROUGH CHAOS)
==========================
• Jayson decides to defuse future university friction: walks to the A-rank table, congratulates them, buys a round.
• Jayson acknowledges fault: admits to baiting, humiliating them; says he doesn’t want “baggage” at university.
• Jayson bows and leaves, then tells Kestrel/Irix they don’t need to worry about that group anymore; offers to let Irix speak to them too if she wants.
• Jayson later goes to the bar for more drinks; one of the A-ranks approaches and talks.
• A separate high-level table tries to sneer / posture; Jayson counters with flirt-chaos:
  - “Kneel and apologize and boom, you pass as a human being.”
  - Plays up being called cute; brushes tail under chin; kisses cheek; provocative banter to disarm them publicly.
• Jayson returns to the party table and explains this tactic is “90% foolproof” because people don’t know how to react.

==========================
“ROYAL” BRAT + 8 KNIGHTS → FORMAL CHALLENGE OUTSIDE
==========================
• Later, at the inn, a loud shiny-armored noble/royalty-adjacent warrior enters with 8 knights, throws weight around, kicks beastfolk out.
• He begins “rounds” through tables; targets a beastkin party behind Jayson’s group (catkin + two female party members).
• He grabs one by tail and ear—public abuse.
• Jayson intervenes: steps in, shoves him aside, catches the catkin, checks she’s okay.
• Knights stance up; the noble loses it, demands a royal/formal challenge.
• The Guild Marshal arrives; Jayson suggests a formal challenge outside under guild witness.
• Jayson declares himself champion for the abused beastkin party; allows the brat + all 8 knights to join to “even” odds.
• Outside fight:
  - Jayson uses tails to hook ankles and swing/slam one knight into two others (3 down fast).
  - Continues with relentless close pressure: shield palmstrike leaving a handprint in iron; more knights dropped rapidly.
• Noble draws a long sword and stabs Jayson through shoulder area (under collarbone). Jayson steps into it deliberately, then bends/crushes his chestplate until he collapses struggling to breathe.
• Jayson rips the front off enough to let him breathe and steps aside for the Marshal to handle arrests/authority.
• Healer removes the sword; it comes out clean but hits a nerve—brief white-hot pain. Jayson jokes throughout to keep the air light.
• Jayson also ensures the earlier catkin ear injury is checked by healer; encourages her not to downplay it.
• Aftermath: Marshal indicates Jayson isn’t in trouble (or limited trouble), and notes Jayson won’t be around much due to university acceptance/trials coming.

==========================
UNIVERSITY ACCEPTANCE LETTER → LEAVE HARROWFEN EARLY
==========================
• Jayson reads acceptance details about reaching the capital and timing.
• Party decides to leave sooner than expected (capital ~4 days travel).
• Jayson spends the day gathering supplies (including buying a bow and arrows).
• They inform the inn they won’t return and notify the guild they’re heading out.
• Cart is loaded, horse prepped; they depart Harrowfen.

==========================
ROAD TO VALENHOLD (CAPITAL) — RACISM RISING
==========================
• Night travel includes sleeping in/near the cart off-road.
• First two days go fine; by day 3, approaching the capital, hostility toward beastkin becomes more open and frequent.
• Midday day 3: warmer weather; they break at a river glade to wash up, cool down, rest horse.
• River scene:
  - Jayson pushes Kestrel and Irix in; then jumps in too.
  - Playful chase: Kestrel “born of water people” energy; Jayson does exaggerated fake drowning / floating face-down.
  - Jayson checks Irix about wings after the water (concern she’s okay).
• Fishing plan:
  - Irix sits on Jayson’s shoulders; spreads wings wide to cast shadow so fish are easier to see.
  - Jayson spears four trout cleanly with tail tips, compensating for refraction; celebrates, nearly knocks Irix off.
• Shore + fire:
  - Towels distributed; Jayson gathers a ridiculous amount of firewood.
  - Fish cooked with gathered herbs; relaxed bonding, talk about the trip and how fast Irix has adapted/confidence grown.
• Back on road: “capital in a few hours; guild check-in; university check-in tomorrow.”

==========================
VALENHOLD APPROACH — OUTER GATE ENTRY
==========================
• Within/near capital roads: racism intensifies (spitting, insults).
• Jayson jokes about “flinging poo also”; party maintains composure.
• Outer gate: long line, guards stretch silence; papers stamped harder than needed, but they are allowed in.

==========================
BEASTKIN FAMILY ATTACKED — SPLIT RESPONSE
==========================
• Shortly after gate/approach (or on capital roads), party encounters a battered beastkin family held up/robbed, father badly injured.
• Jayson orders Irix to stay with cart; asks if Kestrel’s healing can be used on others.
• Kestrel escorts family toward the Guild for aid and records.
• Jayson goes after the thieves alone to retrieve belongings (states he won’t kill them; uses intimidation theatrics).

==========================
“SPIDER NEEDS FOOD” INTIMIDATION → RECOVERY
==========================
• Jayson hunts thieves through alleys, whistles, calls them out, threatens to “release the largest spider.”
• He escalates demands: return goods + also their gold/belongings + their cart.
• Uses tails under coat like spider legs, pretends to calm the “spider.”
• Thieves comply: Jayson loads recovered belongings and a lot of gold, takes the cart, exits back to main roads.

==========================
CAPITAL GATE CHECKPOINT — PAPERS + ACCEPTANCE LETTER
==========================
• At the main gate checkpoint: Jayson claims the items are his/people’s belongings (not trade goods).
• Shows guild badge; presents university acceptance letter; guards let him through.
• Jayson heads for the Guild, worried because he can’t see Kestrel/Irix and the longer it goes, the worse the gut-feeling gets.

==========================
VALENHOLD GUILD HALL — REUNION + FAMILY STABILIZATION
==========================
• Jayson reaches the Guild Quarter; patrol briefly blocks line-of-sight to the Guild doors, increasing tension.
• Reunion at Guild steps: Irix spots Jayson; relief; Kestrel confirms they’re safe and inside.
• A guild hand takes carts/horse arrangements; party follows clerk/healer process with the injured family.
• Jayson lightly distracts the children by poking/tickling them with tail tips, pretending innocence, easing tension in the room.
• Jayson comforts the mother, assures father will be fine, confirms the stolen items are recovered (plus extra).
• Statements given: Jayson reports thieves abandoned the belongings when chased; omits “spider” intimidation details.
• Jayson crouches to the children: promises their father is strong; tells them the world will be better when they grow up.
• Family thanks them; party prepares to leave the stabilization antechamber—end state: they’re in Valenhold, officially recorded, reunited, and positioned for university check-in next.
