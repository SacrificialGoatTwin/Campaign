Scene Title: Two Conversations, One Table
The table becomes its own little world: steam, clinking bowls, torn bread, and the soft roar of a room that’s safely alive.
🦊 YOU | "playful" [🦊]

📝 You absolutely stuff your face, zero dignity, and reach for breadsticks like they’re treasure.

🦊 LIRI | "competitive" [🦊]

📝 Liri mirrors you with gleeful seriousness—elbows in, eyes narrowed, fox toy tucked safely aside so she can commit fully to the war.

💬 “Mine!”

📝 She snatches one just before your fingers get there and giggles with her whole body.

🧍🏽‍♀️ KESTREL | "softened" [🛡️]

📝 Kestrel watches the two of you for a moment—expression caught between disbelief and affection—then exhales a quiet laugh and turns toward Liri’s mother.

💬 “She hasn’t smiled like that in a long time.”

🦊 FOXKIN MOTHER | "fragile relief" [🦊]

📝 The mother’s hands tremble faintly around her bowl. She keeps glancing at Liri like she’s afraid the sight will vanish if she blinks.

💬 “I kept setting out her cup.”

📝 She swallows hard, voice low so it doesn’t crack too loudly.

💬 “Every morning. Like a… stupid ritual.”

🧍🏽‍♀️ KESTREL | "gentle" [🛡️]

📝 Kestrel leans in a fraction—close enough to be heard, not close enough to crowd.

💬 “Not stupid.”

💬 “It’s how you survived the waiting.”

📝 Her eyes soften, and for a second the calm sadness in them shows cleanly.

💬 “I’ve waited like that too.”

🦊 FOXKIN MOTHER | "searching" [🦊]

📝 She studies Kestrel now, really seeing her.

💬 “You’re not from here.”

📝 Not an accusation. A noticing.

💬 “You look like someone who learned to keep moving because stopping hurts.”

🧍🏽‍♀️ KESTREL | "honest" [🛡️]

📝 Kestrel’s fingers rest around her cup, knuckles pale for a breath.

💬 “I used to think if I was useful enough, no one would leave.”

💬 “Turns out… people leave anyway.”

📝 She glances toward you and Liri—still battling for breadsticks—then back.

💬 “But watching him bring her back…”

💬 “It makes the world feel less final.”

🦊 FOXKIN MOTHER | "quiet gratitude" [🦊]

📝 The mother’s eyes shine again, but she keeps her voice steady.

💬 “He’s… different.”

📝 A pause.

💬 “And you—staying beside him. That matters.”

🧍🏽‍♀️ KESTREL | "resolved" [🛡️]

📝 Kestrel nods once, small and certain.

💬 “He doesn’t have to do it alone.”

💬 “Not this time.”

🍞 TABLE (RANDOM TURN — MINOR) | "absurd" [🌬️]

📝 A breadstick snaps in half mid-tug-of-war and launches—landing perfectly on the rim of your bowl like a triumphant banner.

A nearby patron applauds once, dead serious, then returns to eating.

🦊 YOU & 🦊 LIRI | "victorious chaos" [🦊]

📝 The two of you continue your playground war, utterly unaware of the quiet, deep conversation happening two feet away—because some battles are sacred.

The table holds both truths at once:
laughter loud enough to heal,
and softness quiet enough to last.
