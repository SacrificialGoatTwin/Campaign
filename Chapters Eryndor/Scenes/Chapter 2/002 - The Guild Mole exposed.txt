Scene Title: The Name Under the Ink

The buyer’s breath comes in shards as you haul him by the collar. Gravel slips under his good foot; his ruined knee hangs useless. You walk him to the low ledge—stone and drop enough to ruin a body without killing it.

🦊 JAYSON | contained-violence [🦊❄️]

📝 You hold him out over the edge like he’s weightless.

💬 "I am aware the guild is compromised."
💬 "That compromise is why I’m stuck."
💬 "Not taking jobs. Laying low."

Your grip doesn’t shake.

💬 "Tell me who the person in the Guild is."
💬 "The mole."

A pause—flat as a blade.

💬 "Or I will drop you."

🧊 BUYER | fear-turns-to-bargain [😖🩸]

His hands clamp on your wrist, knuckles white. He tries to sneer—fails. He swallows hard, eyes flicking between the ground and your face.

💬 "Y-you’re not…"
💬 "You won’t—"

He stops when you don’t blink.

💬 "Fine—fine!"
💬 "It’s not a captain."
💬 "Not a marshal."

He wets his lips, voice dropping to a hurried whisper.

💬 "Scribe office."
💬 "Second desk that copies writs—"
💬 "—they skim names before they’re pinned."

He squeezes his eyes shut, then blurts:

💬 "Marlen."
💬 "Marlen Voss."
💬 "Handles incoming runner notes—"
💬 "and ‘misfiles’ the ones that matter."

A frantic add-on, like piling details will save him.

💬 "They mark targets with little ink ticks."
💬 "Left corner."
💬 "Looks like a smear."

🧭 KESTREL | immediate-snap-to-procedure [⚔️❄️]

From the slope, Kestrel’s voice cuts in—hard, controlled, not pleading.

💬 "Jayson—don’t."

She doesn’t rush you. She plants herself where she can be seen, eyes locked on the buyer, then on the ledger in your hand.

💬 "Get the name again."
💬 "Slow."
💬 "And you—"

Her gaze pins the buyer like a nail.

💬 "You’re repeating it on record when we reach the Annex."

She shifts her stance, readying rope—practical, not dramatic.

💬 "Drop him and we lose leverage."
💬 "Keep him breathing and we get a chain."

🎲 RANDOM TURN | the-name-has-weight [🌾]

“Marlen Voss” hangs in the cold like smoke that won’t disperse.

If it’s true, it explains the tailored jobs, the eyes, the pressure.
If it’s a lie, it’s a poison pill meant to send you back into the Guild’s throat.

Either way, you’ve forced something real:

A name to test.
A mark to look for.
A thread to pull.

And your hand is still holding him over the edge.
