Scene Title: WHERE THE STEP GOES

The orchard lets you go.

👤 JAYSON | committed [🦊🕯️]

💬 “I’m good.”
💬 “Just about to do something stupid.”
💬 “I’ll be back shortly.”

📝 You square yourself to the dip, understanding settling into your bones: in is a step, not a fall. Whoever came before didn’t know that.

📝 Eyes closed, you lean—

🌑 THE SEAM | transition [⚖️🕳️]

📝 There is vertigo without motion. Falling without direction. Your stomach lags behind your thoughts by a heartbeat that doesn’t finish.

📝 Then it stops.

Not with impact. With arrival.

👁️ THE OTHER SIDE | revealed [🕯️🌫️]

📝 You open your eyes.

You are standing on firm ground—dark loam veined with pale roots that glow faintly, like moonlit bone beneath skin. The air is cool, breathable, and utterly still. Sound exists here, but only after you decide to make it.

📝 Behind you: nothing that looks like a hole. Just a shallow depression that refuses to stay in focus when you stare at it.

📝 Ahead: the orchard—almost. The trees are the same shape, the same spacing, but wrong by degrees. Trunks stretch a little too tall. Leaves hang frozen mid-rustle. Shadows don’t match any light source you can find.

📝 Off to your left, about twenty paces away—

A figure.

Slumped against a tree that shouldn’t exist here. Pack on the ground. One boot missing. Breathing shallow, uneven—but breathing.

✨ THE LIGHTS | boundary keepers [🕯️⚖️]

📝 The motes are here too, hovering at a respectful distance behind you, brighter than before. They do not come closer. They do not retreat.

📝 They have done their part.

🌒 THE MOMENT | truth paid for [🧭🕯️]

📝 The Spark tightens—not power, but clarity.

The person didn’t die.
They didn’t vanish.
They missed the rule.

And now you’re standing in a place that doesn’t want you—but hasn’t decided to keep you yet.

The vertigo is gone.
Your balance is yours again.
And the only way out… is the same way in.

The figure stirs faintly.

Scene Title: A VOICE REACHES HER

The ground doesn’t resist you. It simply accepts each step as if it’s deciding whether to remember them.

👤 JAYSON | urgent care [🦊⚕️]

📝 You dart the twenty paces in a breath, kneeling beside her, keeping your weight low and steady.

💬 “Miss—are you okay?”

👩‍🦱 STRANDED WOMAN | barely conscious [😵‍💫🫁]

📝 Her head lolls against the bark. Lips dry. One hand twitches as if it’s trying to grab a thought that keeps slipping.

💬 “—huh…?”

📝 Her eyes crack open— unfocused, glassy—then drift shut again.

💬 “I… I stepped wrong…”

🌫️ THE PLACE | time misbehaves [🕯️⚖️]

📝 There’s no smell of rot. No cold of death. Just exhaustion—profound, cellular. Like she’s been awake far longer than a body should allow.

📝 Her pack is open. Rations untouched. Water skin still half-full.

📝 She didn’t starve.
📝 She didn’t bleed.
📝 She waited.

✨ THE LIGHTS | watching, not helping [🕯️👁️]

📝 The motes hover at the edge of your awareness, brighter now—but they do not intervene. They won’t carry her. They won’t guide you back.

📝 This part is yours.

🌒 THE MOMENT | stakes clarified [🧭🕯️]

📝 You feel it clearly now:

She’s alive.
She’s rescuable.
But she won’t last much longer here.

And whatever rule lets you leave—
you’ll have to teach it to her fast…
before this place decides she’s late enough to keep.

Her eyes flutter again, struggling to focus on your face.

💬 “Did I… make it back?”

She doesn’t know she hasn’t yet.

Scene Title: TEACHING THE STEP

The place listens harder when you touch her—like it’s counting whether this still belongs to you.

👤 JAYSON | steady reassurance [🦊⚕️]

💬 “Not yet.”
💬 “But thanks to some really weird lights, I came in to find you.”

📝 You slide an arm under her shoulder and help her stand. She’s lighter than she should be, balance uncertain—like gravity keeps checking her paperwork.

📝 You clock the details without stopping: no tail, pointed ears but not elven, not human either. Later. Living comes first.

📝 Her pack comes up and over your shoulder in one smooth motion. You guide her toward the shallow dent that refuses to look the same twice.

💬 “Okay.”
💬 “Here’s the deal.”

📝 You slow your pace, voice lowering—not commanding, just clear.

💬 “Close your eyes.”
💬 “When you feel the pull—like vertigo—”
💬 “Don’t fight it.”
💬 “Let it take you.”

📝 You angle her feet, careful to line her up the way the lights taught you—weight centered, step intentional.

💬 “You’ll come out on the other side.”
💬 “…Hopefully.”

👩‍🦱 STRANDED WOMAN | trust over certainty [😰🫁]

📝 Her breath stutters. She swallows, then nods once—small, fierce.

💬 “I trust you.”

📝 She closes her eyes. Her fingers tighten in your sleeve—not clinging, just checking that you’re real.

✨ THE LIGHTS | permission granted [🕯️⚖️]

📝 The motes brighten together and drift closer—not touching, not guiding—aligning. The air at the dent thins again, more decisive this time.

📝 The pull arrives—gentle, insistent—like the world offering to finish a sentence it started.

🌑 THE SEAM | rule demonstrated [⚖️🕳️]

📝 Vertigo blooms without motion. Her grip tightens—then steadies as she stops resisting.

📝 For a breath that doesn’t belong to any clock, the ground forgets her.

💬 “—I’m back.”
