Scene Title: WHAT THE STONE REMEMBERS

Chalk dust hangs in sunbeams. Boots scuff. Pins clink. The board creaks as fresh parchment settles into place.

🦊 JAYSON | looking ahead [🦊🌅]

💬 “Early bird catches the worm.”
💬 “Any dungeons… or ruins by chance?”
💬 “If not, I’ll take a look at the board.”

📝 You angle slightly, eyes already skimming titles and seals.

🧾 GUILD CLERK | measured options [🙂📜]

📝 The clerk lifts a brow, flips a page, then taps two notices apart from the main cluster.

💬 “Dungeons—officially?”
💬 “None opened yet.”

A pause, then—

💬 “But.”

They slide one parchment closer.

💬 “Collapsed culvert outside the old quarry.”
💬 “Stonework predates the canal.”
💬 “We’re calling it salvage until someone proves otherwise.”

Another tap, farther down the board.

💬 “And a ruin.”
💬 “Watchtower foundations north of the reed flats.”
💬 “No monsters reported.”
💬 “Which means either it’s clean…”
💬 “…or no one came back to report.”

They look up at you.

💬 “Your rank allows solo registration.”
💬 “Mark intent and time window.”

🎲 RANDOM TURN | interest stirs [⚠️👀]

📝 A pair of adventurers nearby go quiet, pretending not to listen.
📝 Someone else reaches for the ruin notice—then hesitates when they see your badge.

🧭 THE BOARD | choices with weight [🌤️]

📝 Salvage that might be older than the city’s memory.
📝 A ruin that’s been polite enough to stay quiet.

Both are still there.
For now.

The chalk waits for your mark.

Scene Title: CLAIMS AND COINCIDENCE

Chalk squeaks. Paper rustles. The board smells faintly of glue and old rain. A few heads turn—just enough to notice, not enough to stare.

🦊 JAYSON | open offer [🦊🌅]

💬 “If you’d like it, please feel free.”
💬 “Or we can team up, if you want.”

📝 Your gaze settles briefly on the woman’s hand hovering near the notice—no pressure, just an opening.

💬 “I’ll take them both if she doesn’t want the one or to join.”

📝 You turn back to the desk, casual as anything.

🗺️ ADVENTURER – Kestrel Thorne | weighing risk [😐⚖️]

📝 She snorts softly, withdrawing her hand and finally meeting your eyes. Sun catches the scar at her temple; her pin is old, edges worn smooth.

💬 “Wasn’t hesitating.”
💬 “Just deciding if today was the day I felt lucky.”

A beat—then a shrug.

💬 “Ruin north of the flats?”
💬 “I’ll pass solo.”

She taps the parchment with two fingers.

💬 “But I’ll walk it with you.”
💬 “Safer to have someone watching your back who isn’t trying to impress you.”

🧾 GUILD CLERK – Orrin Bale | ink moves fast [🙂📜]

📝 Orrin’s already writing, charcoal scratching decisively.

💬 “One solo salvage—culvert.”
💬 “One partnered reconnaissance—reed flats tower.”

He glances up, confirming.

💬 “Team of two for the ruin.”
💬 “Kestrel Thorne and Jayson.”

A stamp thuds.

💬 “Windows marked.”
💬 “Bring back anything that looks like it remembers being important.”

🎲 RANDOM TURN | the board reacts [⚠️👂]

📝 A loud argument erupts near the door about rations.
📝 A courier drops a satchel; papers scatter.
📝 Someone laughs too hard—nerves, not humor.

The hall keeps moving.

🧭 THE HALL | lines drawn [🌤️]

📝 Two jobs claimed.
📝 One partner chosen by chance, not fate.
📝 The day finally decides it wants you outside.

Kestrel rolls her shoulders and nods toward the doors.

💬 “Reed flats first.”

The chalk dries. The city waits.
