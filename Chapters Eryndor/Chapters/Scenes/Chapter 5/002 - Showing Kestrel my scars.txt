Scene Title: What Scars Are For
The alcove feels smaller—not cramped, just focused. Warm stone holds the heat steady while the sigils dim to a respectful glow, as if they know better than to compete.

📝 You look down at the scars and smile—soft, private.

🦊 JAYSON | reflective [🦊]

💬 “Some scars you keep as a reminder.”

💬 “Others you keep out of respect.”

📝 You point to the one along your side.

🦊 JAYSON | candid [🦊]

💬 “I did some regrettable things around this time.”

💬 “This one reminds me of what I almost lost.”

💬 “What I almost lost is unrelated to the scar… but it was around the same time.”

📝 A pause. Then you gesture to the others.

🦊 JAYSON | steady [🦊]

💬 “One of my family is Wolfkin.”

💬 “Not Foxkin.”

💬 “To let her be part of my family without angering the rest of the wolfkin—”

💬 “As some god who takes what he wants…”

📝 You glance at her, meeting her eyes.

🦊 JAYSON | firm [🦊]

💬 “Which was not the case.”

📝 Your gaze returns to the scars.

🦊 JAYSON | resolute [🦊]

💬 “I challenged one of their strongest.”

💬 “I stripped my godhood and fought fox to wolf.”

💬 “I kept the scars for two reasons.”

💬 “One—so I remember what I fought for.”

💬 “Two—out of respect for the wolf I fought.”

💬 “To show them that being a god didn’t make me less of a man… or a fox.”

🛡️ KESTREL | moved [🛡️]

📝 She doesn’t speak at first. Her eyes rest on the scars—not counting, not judging—recognizing the shape of an earned truth.

💬 “That’s…”

💬 “Proper.”

📝 The word lands clean. Warrior to warrior.

💬 “Where I’m from, scars kept on purpose mean you stood your ground.”

💬 “And paid for it.”

📝 She sits back, shoulders easing, respect settled rather than displayed.
