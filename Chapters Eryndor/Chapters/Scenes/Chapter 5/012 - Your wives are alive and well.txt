🌅 HARROWFEN — MARKETWARD INN (JAYSON’S ROOM) | ☁️ OVERCAST | 🌄 DAWN, 06:13
Scene Title: 24/04/1427 — Proof Without Sight
Morning light inches across the floorboards. Dust drifts lazily in the beam like it has all the time in the world. The inn is awake, but this room is very still.

📝 You stand there for a long time, staring at the paper.

📝 Long enough for anger to cool.
📝 Long enough for hope to hurt.

📝 You pick it up.

📝 You open it.

The paper is warm.

Not heat—presence.

The writing is not ink.

It is memory pressed into shape.

Four lines.
Four hands, unmistakable.

Written in Kitsune, the old way—no wasted symbols, no embellishment.

📝 The first line is sharp, precise. Ayaka.

“Still standing. Still burning. Do not rush what must be endured.”

📝 The second is clean, disciplined, restrained to the edge of pain. Shion.

“Alive. Hidden by choice. Your patience protects us more than your rage ever could.”

📝 The third is chaotic, affectionate, furious that it had to be brief. Seika.

“OI. Took you long enough. Don’t you dare die before we kick your ass for worrying us.”

📝 The last line is gentle. Steady. Anchoring. Lysera.

“We are intact. We are together when it matters. Come to us whole.”

RANDOM TURN — Difficulty 9

📝 The paper crumbles into nothing the moment the last line finishes being understood.

📝 Not burned.
📝 Not destroyed.
📝 Released.

📝 The warmth fades.

📝 Outside, a cart wheel hits a rut and laughs it off.

📝 Somewhere down the hall, a door opens. Footsteps pass.

📝 The world continues—unaware it just survived something.

📝 You are alone in the room again.

📝 But not uncertain.

📝 Not anymore.
