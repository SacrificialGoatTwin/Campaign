Scene Title: When the Adrenaline Lets Go
The room is quiet in the way only aftermath can be. Clean sheets. A single lantern. The muffled sound of the inn returning to life below—chairs scraping, doors closing, people pretending nothing happened.

📝 The three of you sit in the paid-for room.

📝 Not close.

📝 Not far.

📝 Just… present.

📝 Fifteen minutes pass.

📝 No one pushes the silence.

🎭 Irix | "fractured" [🎭]

📝 It starts small.

📝 Her hands—resting in her lap—begin to shake.

📝 She presses them together, hard, like she can force stillness by will alone.

💬 “I—”

📝 Her voice catches.

📝 She swallows.

💬 “I thought I was used to it.”

📝 Breath stutters once. Then again.

💬 “Running.”

💬 “Changing names.”

💬 “Being careful.”

📝 Her shoulders draw inward.

💬 “I did everything right.”

📝 The words fracture on the last syllable.

💬 “And they still found me.”

📝 Her breathing breaks—fast, shallow.

💬 “They always do.”

📝 She curls forward, horns angled down, hands finally coming up to cover her face.

💬 “I don’t want to be important.”

💬 “I don’t want to matter to them.”

💬 “I just want—”

📝 Her voice collapses into a sound she’s been holding back all night.

📝 A sob—raw, exhausted, uncontrolled.

🛡️ Kestrel | "gentle-guard" [🛡️]

📝 Kestrel moves first—not fast.

📝 She lowers herself beside Irix, shield set aside, armor quiet.

📝 One hand settles on Irix’s back—firm, grounding.

💬 “You don’t have to hold it together here.”

📝 The lantern flickers once, then steadies.

📝 Outside, a laugh rises and fades—someone else’s normal life, continuing.

📝 Inside, the room becomes a place where the body finally believes the danger has passed.

🎭 Irix | "unravelling" [🎭]

💬 “I was so scared.”

💬 “When they grabbed me—”

📝 She chokes on the words, breath hitching.

💬 “I thought… this was it.”

📝 Her fingers clutch at Kestrel’s sleeve without realizing it.

💬 “I didn’t even get angry.”

💬 “I just… felt tired.”

💬 “I hate that part the most.”

📝 The room holds.

📝 Not tense.

📝 Not urgent.

📝 Just… steady.

And far more important.
