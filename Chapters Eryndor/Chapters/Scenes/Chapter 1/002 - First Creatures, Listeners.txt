Scene Title: ECHOES WALK AWAY

Mist coils where sound used to live, reluctant to give the forest back its silence.

👤 JAYSON | controlled [🪨🧠]

📝 One last stone leaves your hand—thrown farther this time, angled to travel. It hammers another trunk deep in the woods. The echo hops tree to tree, a convincing retreat.

📝 You wait a beat. Then drop from the branch—landing light despite the ache—and turn to the man.

💬 “What… the fuck… were those things?”

🧍‍♂️ STRANGER | rattled [😨🌲]

📝 He flinches at your voice, then swallows hard. Mud streaks his cloak; his boots are torn to the sole.

💬 “Listeners.”
💬 “Stonebacks, some call them—if they’re being polite.”

📝 He keeps his eyes moving as he talks, never settling on one patch of fog for long.

💬 “They don’t hunt you.”
💬 “They hunt mistakes.”

💬 “Noise. Panic. Bad footing.”
💬 “You make one wrong sound and they finish the map.”

📝 His jaw tightens, shame and relief mixing.

💬 “You don’t kill them early.”
💬 “You survive them… or you don’t.”

🌲 THE FOREST | watching [👁️🍃]

📝 Far away, the clicking fades—then stops. Not gone. Satisfied.

📝 The Spark settles again, barely perceptible, as if tallying another line: risk taken, outcome altered. No reward yet—only memory.

📝 The man finally looks straight at you, really looks.

💬 “You’re not from any border I know.”
💬 “And you don’t smell like the rest.”

📝 He gestures deeper into the trees, away from where the echoes traveled.

💬 “If you want to live past noon…”
💬 “We should keep moving.”

The forest offers no objection.
