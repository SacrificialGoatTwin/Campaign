CAMPAIGN SUMMARY
CHAPTER 7 — ERYNDOR

==========================

DATE & TIME
-----------
• Date: 26/04/1427 (Eryndor calendar)
• Time: Morning → Late Night
• Weather: Persistent rain, easing toward night
• Primary Locations:
  - Harrowfen (Guild Hall, Tailor, Market District)
  - Large central inn hosting adventurers & university scouts
  - Court Park (near the Guild)
  - Copper Finch Inn (party’s lodging)

PARTY
-----
• Jayson (PC) — Kitsune anomaly, diminished power, extreme restraint/lethality
• Kestrel — C-rank adventurer, disciplined, observant, trusted ally
• Irix — Serathi (misidentified by many as “demon”), continuity anchor, wings hidden, new Guild protection

==========================
MORNING & DAY EVENTS
==========================

• Party wakes at Copper Finch Inn after assassination attempt the night before.
• Jayson reassures Irix about her horns and healing; swelling is gone.
• Party heads to Guild Hall to report the assassination attempt.
• Racist remarks from other adventurers occur; Jayson deflects with humor and provocation rather than violence.
• Marshal interviews the party:
  - Unknown religiously dressed figures attempted to “verify” Irix.
  - Assassins identified as a professional guild outside Guild authority.
  - No emblems, strong confidence, organized withdrawal.
• Jayson openly declares Irix under his protection.
• Party plans to stay in Harrowfen due to rumors of University of Magic recruiters.

==========================
TAILOR & Irix’s NEW CLOTHING
==========================

• Party visits a tailor experienced with non-humans.
• Irix receives:
  - Custom clothing for digigrade legs, tail, and wings
  - Comfortable footwear designed to protect pads while leaving claws free
• Jayson is openly fascinated by Irix’s feet (playful, non-sexual curiosity).
• Tailor crafts items same-day using dungeon leather provided by Jayson.
• Jayson commissions cloaks for Kestrel and Irix and tips generously.
• Irix gains visible confidence from clothing that finally fits her body.

==========================
UNIVERSITY HALL & SPARK USE
==========================

• Party waits in a massive hall filled with mana and high-ranking adventurers.
• Jayson upgrades Spark:
  - Chooses Hearthbound and Heatwave.
• Jayson experiments with Hearth magic:
  - Discovers it can act as a thermal container.
  - Learns to heat and cool air inside controlled zones.
• Jayson experiments with mana mechanics:
  - Creates a leather “filter” that condenses ambient mana into liquid form.
  - Produces extremely pure white/silver mana.
  - Uses it to power a self-made stone interface (holographic projection of party).
• Jayson realizes this level of mana manipulation would draw attention and destroys the filter afterward.

==========================
MARKET & ESCALATING HOSTILITY
==========================

• Party walks through market district under Hearth rain-shield.
• Irix receives heavy stares and racist comments due to her exposed appearance.
• Jayson intervenes with theatrical provocation instead of violence.
• Jayson gifts Irix a horn ornament, custom-shaped and optional.
• Mood remains light, but hostility around them increases.

==========================
THE INN INCIDENT (MAJOR ESCALATION)
==========================

• Party enters a massive inn crowded with high-ranking adventurers and scouts.
• Racism toward beastfolk and Serathi escalates.
• Jayson uses:
  - Sexualized mockery
  - Literal interpretation of insults
  - Social humiliation
• A known high-ranking adventuring group becomes openly hostile.
• They attempt intimidation, then surround the party.

==========================
COURT PARK CONFRONTATION
==========================

• Hostile adventurers lure the party outside.
• Their intent becomes explicit:
  - Threats to drain Irix’s mana (equivalent to blood).
  - Threats to break her legs.
  - Threats to make Jayson watch.
• Jayson shifts from joking to silent, controlled violence.
• Combat:
  - Jayson disables multiple attackers non-lethally.
  - Splits the leader’s steel chest plate cleanly in half.
  - Uses tails and terrain as weapons.
  - Kestrel controls flanks and disarms attackers.
• City guards arrive mid-confrontation.
• Jayson de-escalates verbally:
  - Frames the scene as an apology.
  - Forces the aggressors to kneel and apologize to Irix.
• Guards order all aggressors to report to the Guild Marshal at first bell.
• Incident is officially recorded.

==========================
RETURN TO COPPER FINCH INN
==========================

• Party returns safely to their rooms.
• Jayson apologizes to Irix for frightening her.
• Lighthearted teasing about her height follows.
• Jayson collapses onto the floor from exhaustion.

==========================
INTIMATE, QUIET CHARACTER MOMENTS
==========================

• Jayson removes boots, relaxes, and openly admires:
  - Kestrel’s ears
  - Irix’s paws
• Boundaries and consent are explicitly discussed:
  - Kitsune tails are sacred and soul-linked.
  - Jayson is an exception and allows free contact.
• Irix reveals her wings privately:
  - Functional for short flight and gliding.
  - Culturally significant—symbol of rank, bloodline, and expectation.
• Jayson explains Kitsune tail culture:
  - Tails earned through trials.
  - Born with 9 tails (extremely rare).
  - Gained a 10th, later sacrificed to create a homeland for his people.
• Mutual understanding forms between Jayson and Irix regarding bodily symbolism and burden.

==========================
EMOTIONAL RESOLUTION
==========================

• Jayson declares his long-term intent:
  - To make Eryndor safe from discrimination and hate once his power returns.
  - This is the reason he was sent to this world.
• Kestrel acknowledges belief in him.
• Irix feels seen and respected rather than targeted or hidden.

==========================
NIGHT & SLEEP
==========================

• Jayson falls asleep mid-conversation from exhaustion.
• Irix and Kestrel talk privately:
  - Pasts, family, conditional love, survival strategies.
  - No discussion about Jayson.
  - Mutual trust forms.
• Jayson sleep-talks nonsense involving:
  - Dumplings
  - Fish wagons
  - Rats that eat stress
• His tails drape protectively over both women.
• Eventually, Jayson enters deep REM sleep.
• Ward settles into night-cycle.
• Scene ends in safety, calm, and trust.
