============================
THE ARCANUM UNIVERSITAS OF VALENHOLD
============================

🏛️ Official Name:
The Arcanum Universitas of Valenhold

Common Names:
The University
The Arcanum
The Axiom

🏰 Location:
Capital City of Valenhold, Nyambuza

The University occupies an entire fortified district of Valenhold
and extends miles below it.

Valenhold exists because of the University.
The University does not depend on Valenhold.

============================
FOUNDATION & TRUE PURPOSE
============================

📜 Founding Era:
Late Second Era, post–Ember Uprising

📖 Public Mandate:
- Education of magic users
- Preservation of knowledge
- Regulation of dangerous disciplines
- Defense against existential threats

📖 Actual Mandate:
- Control the distribution of power
- Train operatives, not scholars
- Maintain monopoly over advanced magic
- Act as military, intelligence, and research command
- Prepare for wars not yet allowed to exist

The University does not react to history.
It prepares replacements for it.

============================
PHYSICAL STRUCTURE
============================

🏛️ Campus Nature:
A fortified arcane–military district built on engineered leyline convergence.

🧱 Major Structures:

• The Prime Axiom  
Central spire and command nexus.
Contains:
- High Convocation chambers
- Strategic divination arrays
- Leyline command engine
- World-scale failsafes

• Faculty & Doctrine Rings  
Not organized by magic alone — by function.

• War Colleges  
Dedicated academies for military doctrine, command, and force application.

• The Deep Stacks  
Archive, vault, prison, laboratory, and reliquary complex.

• The Black Sublevels  
Officially nonexistent.
Contain:
- God-fragments
- Erased histories
- Failed Ascendants
- Things no longer considered people

============================
STUDENT TIERS (7 LEVELS)
============================

🎓 Tier I — Initiates  
Observation, conditioning, theory.
High attrition is intentional.

🎓 Tier II — Novices  
Basic spellcasting and discipline exposure.
Constant monitoring.

🎓 Tier III — Adepts  
Declared specialization.
Simulations and controlled field work.

🎓 Tier IV — Arcanists  
Licensed operatives.
Live deployments.
Military integration begins.

🎓 Tier V — Magisters  
Command-capable.
Lead squads, research cells, or doctrine teams.

🎓 Tier VI — Savants  
Exceptional individuals.
Access to:
- Deep Stacks
- Strategic magic
- Classified weapons programs

🎓 Tier VII — Ascendants  
Rare.
No longer fully mortal.
Candidates for:
- Faculty replacement
- Black Programs
- Convocation succession

Leaving is optional.
Surviving is not guaranteed.

============================
STUDENT PECKING ORDER
============================

The University claims meritocracy.

In practice, an unofficial hierarchy known as
**“The Natural Gradient”**
governs survival, access, and advancement.

From highest to lowest:

1. High Elves & pure arcane bloodlines  
2. Human highborn & sponsored nobility  
3. Humans (unsponsored)  
4. Dwarves & sanctioned non-humans  
5. Mixed-bloods  
6. Foreigners & political externs  
7. **Beastfolk (ALL TYPES)**

----------------------------
BEASTFOLK STATUS
----------------------------

Beastfolk are considered:
- Intellectually unstable
- Spiritually volatile
- Acceptable losses

Institutional realities:
- Inferior housing
- Restricted archives
- Mandatory “safety” glyphs
- Harsher punishment
- Used as test subjects
- First deployed, last extracted

Their failure is expected.
Their success is resented.

The University tracks which Beastfolk:
- Break
- Die
- Adapt too well

----------------------------
UNOFFICIAL USE OF BEASTFOLK
----------------------------

Common funnels:
- Shock troops
- Hazard clearance
- Monster handling
- Experimental augmentation
- Disposable reconnaissance

Survival is proof.
Death is data.

============================
SUPREME LEADERSHIP
============================

Five individuals sit above all others.

They are not administrators.
They are deterrents.

----------------------------
THE HEADMISTRESS
----------------------------

Bearer of the Prime Seal.
Absolute internal authority.
One of the most powerful magic users alive.

Capabilities include:
- Leyline overwriting
- Reality anchoring
- Population-scale memory excision
- God-fragment containment

When she appears, events stop.

----------------------------
THE DEPUTY HEADMASTER
----------------------------

Master of Strategic Operations.
Warden of Black Programs.

Equal in power to the Headmistress in some domains.
More dangerous in others.

If the Headmistress is restraint,
the Deputy is execution.

----------------------------
THE THREE HOUSES
----------------------------

HOUSE I — THE AXIOMATIC HOUSE  
Order, law, reality enforcement.  
Produces wardens and world-anchors.

HOUSE II — THE TRANSCENDENT HOUSE  
Ascension, transformation, post-mortal theory.  
Produces Ascendants and living weapons.

HOUSE III — THE VEILED HOUSE  
Secrets, memory warfare, erasure.  
Produces spies, reality editors, silent kings.

Each House Leader is considered
a planetary-level strategic entity.
